# Authors and lineage

Original city simulation: Will Wright / Maxis / Electronic Arts.

Open-source Micropolis release, Unix/Tcl work and subsequent stewardship:
Don Hopkins and contributors named in [__README_OG](__README_OG).

Native C++ / SDL port, Micropolis-SDLPP: Leeor Dicker and its contributors.
The complete inherited Git history and [original SDLPP README](project/reference/README_SDLPP.md)
are preserved. The audited baseline is `9c4e85a0decd57ba6f76d9e1ec82461940ecc3ad`.

Civic 89 modernisation, build/release tooling, procedural sound and geometric
city application mark: Civic 89 contributors (2026). See Git history for authorship.

Raleway fonts: Matt McInerney, Pablo Impallari, Rodrigo Fuenzalida and contributors;
the copyright and SIL Open Font License are in `res/licenses/raleway/OFL.txt`.

Dependency authors and licences are recorded in the packaged
`licenses/dependencies/<package>/copyright` files. Asset provenance and unresolved
inherited icon attribution follow-ups are recorded in `assets/ASSET-LICENSES.yml`
and [the asset investigation](project/ASSET_LICENSE_AUDIT.md).
